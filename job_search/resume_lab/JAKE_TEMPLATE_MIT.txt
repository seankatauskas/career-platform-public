Jake's Resume template
=====================

Author: Jake Gutierrez
Based on Sourabh Bajaj's resume: https://github.com/sb2nov/resume
Source: the Jake Gutierrez template supplied by the user, whose header identifies
the MIT license. The bundled template preserves its layout and custom commands.
Adaptations replace example content with escaped fields and omit pdfTeX-specific
Unicode setup when compiling with Tectonic/XeTeX.
